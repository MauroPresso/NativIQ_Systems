# NativIQ Systems

**AI-native systems for real business operations.**

Public repository documenting the strategic, technical and security foundations of NativIQ Systems.

## Strategic Focus

NativIQ Systems focuses on three complementary areas:

1. **Secure AI Data Systems**
2. **AI Workflow Automation for B2B Operations**
3. **AI Security & AI Governance**

## Repository Structure

- `00_Brand_Evidence` — Brand creation and identity evidence.
- `01_Brand_Strategy` — Strategic positioning, niche selection and company direction.
- `02_Technical_Architecture` — Technical architecture, AI stack and infrastructure research.
- `03_Security_Governance` — Security architecture, AI governance, risk management and Secure SDLC.
- `04_Content_Ideas` — Research notes and future content ideas.
- `05_Scripts` — Scripts and structured content drafts.
- `06_Assets` — Logos, visual assets and templates.
- `07_Published_Content` — Records of published material.

## Engineering Principles

NativIQ Systems is being designed around:

- AI-native architecture
- secure-by-design systems
- model and provider portability
- structured and auditable data
- human-in-the-loop controls
- least privilege
- reproducibility
- responsible automation
- explicit operational controls

## Status

NativIQ Systems is currently in its research, architecture and early development stage.
